ToDo

One list of categories (Work, Home, Church, …) and tasks, nested as deep as you like. Changes save and sync on their own.

Tasks

  • Checkbox: click for done (), click again for to do. Shift-click (or press and hold on a phone) for in progress ().
  • A task shows in progress by itself when any of its subtasks is started or done, until you mark it done.
  • Bold (⌘B) marks a task as top priority. It stays in view even when its category is collapsed (but not when the category above that is collapsed).
  • Descriptions: select a task and click the + to its right. The ▸ next to a name shows or hides it; click in it to edit. ⌘B ⌘I ⌘U format text there, and typing “- ” starts a bulleted list.
  • Tasks checked off stay in view until midnight. Show task history (the clock button) shows them all, with Created / Started / Completed dates you can click to change. See Journal (the book button) lists finished tasks by day.
  • Drag rows to reorder (drop on a row's top or bottom edge) or to move them inside another row (drop on its middle). On a phone, press and hold a row to pick it up.

Typing names (like a checklist in a document)

ReturnSave and start the next task below. At the very start of a name, opens a new task above it instead. On an empty task, outdents it or ends the list.
⇧ReturnWith the cursor in the middle of a name, splits it: the rest becomes the next task. At the end of a name, starts a subtask.
Tab ⇧TabIndent / outdent
↑ ↓Previous / next task
⌫On an empty task, removes it
⌘BTop priority (bold) on / off
EscFinish (or click elsewhere). Empty tasks disappear; spaces at either end of a name are trimmed.
